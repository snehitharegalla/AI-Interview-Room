Dynamic Orchestration Engine

Adaptive Interviewing: How the Engine Decides

Rather than simplistic binary rules (right = harder / wrong = easier), our engine analyzes conceptual depth, pinpointing why an answer succeeded or fell short.

Strong Understanding ACTION: DEEPER

Architectural Escalation

Candidate accurately articulates principles and runtime tradeoffs. The engine pushes into internal mechanics, high concurrency, or steps up difficulty tier.

Q1 (Basic) ➔ Strong response ➔ Q2 (Intermediate: Edge cases & metadata preservation)
Partial Understanding ACTION: PROBE_MISSING

Surgical Gap Probe

Candidate grasps the high-level concept but omits a critical mechanism. The engine crafts a targeted question testing that exact omitted concept without demoting.

Missed LRU eviction details ➔ Q: Focus specifically on doubly-linked list node removal
First Stumble / Weak ACTION: DIAGNOSTIC

Stumble Protection

A candidate who struggles on one difficult question is NOT immediately demoted. The engine issues a foundational diagnostic probe to verify true baseline grasp.

Stumbled on B-Tree node split ➔ Q: Explore fundamental tree traversal before adjusting
Repeated Evidence ACTION: EASIER / PIVOT

Evidence-Based Adjustment

Only after gathering repeated evidence of a topic gap does the engine step down difficulty to establish a baseline, or gracefully pivot to another focus area.

2+ failed diagnostic probes ➔ Step down to fundamentals or pivot to Data Structures

Depth-Weighted Fair Scoring

Questions are weighted by difficulty tier (1.0x Basic, 1.5x Intermediate, 2.2x Advanced). A candidate who excels only on elementary questions cannot achieve an inflated Senior score.

Weighted Score Formula: ∑(score × weight) / ∑(10 × weight) × 10
